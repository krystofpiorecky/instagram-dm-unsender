# Instagram DM Unsender

## ⚠️ Warning ⚠️
Works now (May 2025) but will most likely break in the near future :D It's still a solid and simple starting point for anyone to adjust it when you need it (as i did) 🫡
(tested with TamperMonkey)

## How to
A userscript that will unsend your Instagram messages. 

A blue button will appear after u pressed a chat and reloaded it. Press the blue button and it will automatically unsend all your messages.

USE TAMPERMONKEY OR GREASEMONKEY!

original repo: https://github.com/DirectGames/Instagram-DM-unsender
